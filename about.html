Thiền — slow down, breathe, and hear one gentle line a day

A free bilingual (Vietnamese–English) meditation app. A soft voice, instrumental music, a meditation bell and a breathing guide — fully offline, no ads, no account needed.

Open the web app

Who is it for?

  • You want to unwind after a stressful day or take a quiet few minutes before sleep.
  • You are new to meditation and want something simple and pressure-free: press start and a gentle voice and music begin.
  • You enjoy positive affirmations and reflective lines in Vietnamese or English.

What’s inside

  • Vietnamese and English voice for 50 affirmations and 600+ reflections, read slowly with silences from 12 seconds to 5 minutes.
  • 8 instrumental tracks: meditation and relaxing classical, with smooth crossfades.
  • A three-stroke meditation bell, or a “Relax” mode with no bell.
  • 4-7-8 breathing guide and 6 soft colour themes.
  • Timer, meditation log with streaks, and a daily reminder.
  • It remembers where you left off in the lines and music.

Start in one minute

  1. Open the app and pick Meditation or Relax · Classical.
  2. Set the time (10 minutes by default) and the voice language.
  3. Press ▶, get comfortable, and breathe slowly with the circle.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and no paid tier.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The voice and music are bundled in the app (Android version), so it plays without a connection.

Does it collect my data?

No account is needed. Your log and settings stay on your device. Sign-in and anonymous stats are optional — see the privacy policy.

Is this a medical treatment?

No. It is for relaxation and mindfulness practice. If you are struggling with your mental health, please seek professional help.
